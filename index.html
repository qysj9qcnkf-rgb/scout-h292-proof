  1. Mia's AI fails (after one retry in the same workspace, never another key).
  2. AI and mic off, the page shows “Code unlocked”.
  3. One line, spoken by her voice only (no AI needed).
  4. Call ends; the call is saved with a brain_error event.
  5. Alert email to the team, and the code is minted however few words the shopper said. The store is not billed for the call.
